Animated Poster

Animated posters never found their way into any artwork database or API and remained a fan project.
Most available posters can be found on the Animated Poster Project page.
@moulfo was the main content creator of the project, producing the highest-quality posters in the community.
He sadly passed away in 2020. All credit for this art style goes to him.
